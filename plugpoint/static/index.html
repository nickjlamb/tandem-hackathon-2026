About PlugPoint

PlugPoint turns the documented plan from a clinic consultation into tracked actions, helping ensure investigations, results and follow-up don’t fall through the gaps.

How it works

  1. 1
    ExtractAI identifies investigations and follow-up from the clinic note.
  2. 2
    CheckDeterministic rules verify that the plan is complete and internally consistent.
  3. 3
    ApproveA clinician reviews and approves the plan before anything is actioned.
  4. 4
    TrackPlugPoint monitors outstanding results and follow-up until the loop is closed.

FAQs

Does PlugPoint make clinical decisions?
No. Clinical decisions remain with the clinician. PlugPoint extracts, checks, executes and tracks the documented plan.
Does it automatically order tests?
Only after clinician approval. In this demo, downstream EPR, ordering, booking and messaging systems are simulated.
What happens if a result doesn’t arrive?
The tracker identifies overdue actions and can escalate them rather than allowing the follow-up loop to disappear.
Is the AI trusted to get everything right?
No. AI handles language extraction. Deterministic checks and clinician approval provide separate safeguards before actions are executed.
